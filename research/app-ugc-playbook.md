# Consumer apps promoted with AI-made UGC (Oct 2026)

## What is working (evidence)
- Cal AI: AI calorie scanner, about $40M revenue, driven by 250-300 creators on
  retainer (real people using the app); copycat Calo Scan AI reached about
  $50K MRR with organic TikTok (187 videos, 5M views).
- Pingo AI (language speaking practice): 3M users, about $500K/month, 1B+ views,
  demo-feedback format, copied across languages. Others: Gauth, Yope, MinutesAI.
- RevenueCat 2026: hard paywalls convert about 5x better than freemium (10.7% vs
  2.1% download-to-paid by day 35); AI apps earn about 41% more per payer but
  churn about 30% faster; weekly plans are about 55% of revenue; weekly + trial
  is the highest-LTV paywall; annual plans retain 3-10x better than weekly.
- Sensor Tower: Gen AI app revenue +232% YoY; consumer spend on apps $167B in 2025.
- Higgsfield: a 15-second UGC-style ad is about $4.50 in credits; plans $19-99/mo.

## Risks and guardrails
- Apple Guideline 4.3 (spam / saturated categories) and 4.2 (minimum
  functionality) reject AI-built clones; tightened June 2026. Need a real wedge.
- FTC fake-review rule (Oct 2024): AI avatars are allowed, but an AI "customer"
  giving a first-person experience claim is a fake testimonial. Meta and TikTok
  require AI-content disclosure. Use demo/explainer style with a disclosed AI
  presenter. Never fake "I lost 10 lbs" testimonials.
- Meta restricts health claims and before/after imagery.

## Concepts checked (all crowded or hard)
Thrift/resale scanners (8+ apps), AI closet/outfit (Whering free), bedtime-story
apps (TaleHero, StoryKit, Storynite, BedtimeStory.ai), interview practice (Huru,
Careerflow, Yoodli, Final Round), voice budgeting (Peggy, MonAI, Finny, Cleo),
lifting form-check (GymSpotter, FormLens, Gymscore, Lifted, +3), heritage-language
apps (Yapr 47 languages, Dialect, Tahanan, Langmitra), AI micro-drama (market
$14B but platforms own the audience; content cheap, distribution expensive).

## Best variant so far (unverified demand)
Calorie scanner for cuisines general apps get wrong. Documented gap: AI counters
over/under-estimate ethnic dishes by up to about 50-70%; Southeast Asian food
largely absent from Western databases; sinigang off by 20-30%, pho over by 49%.
Regional rivals exist (Welling for SE Asia, Nutrimate/HealthifyMe for India).
Moat would be a verified dish database for one diaspora. Meta health-claim limits
apply. Test with organic demo-style videos and a fake-door page before building.
