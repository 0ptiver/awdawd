# Amazon FBA research (Oct 2026)

## Numbers (treat survey stats as upper bounds; sellers who quit are not surveyed)
- Jungle Scout 2026: new seller sign-ups at a decade low (165,000); new sellers
  average roughly $30K annual profit; most spend about $2,500-5,000 to start;
  cheap "trinket" selling is effectively dead; mid/high ticket ($20-150) works better.
- Other 2026 roundups: 86-87% of sellers profitable, 63% profitable in year one,
  30% of new sellers under $500/month for the first six months; target 20-30% net margin.

## 2026 cost changes
- FBA fulfillment fees up about $0.08/unit on average (Jan 15); 3.5% fuel surcharge
  (Apr 17, about $0.17/unit); inbound placement fee restructured ($0.14-1.90/unit);
  aged-inventory surcharges from 181 days. Referral fees unchanged.
- Tariffs: Supreme Court invalidated IEEPA tariffs (Feb 20, 2026); 10% Section 122
  global tariff (Feb 24 - Jul 24) replaced by a Section 301 "forced labour" tariff
  (12.5% China/Vietnam, 10% others). Existing China Section 301 (25%+) remains,
  so typical duty above MFN on Chinese goods is about 37.5%.

## Risks
- New accounts get suspended even with no sales (identity video verification,
  mismatched details); account-integrity suspensions recover only 20-40% of the time.
- Review manipulation, inauthentic items, and category gating are common triggers.

## Upside for us
- Brand Referral Bonus: about 10% of sale price credited back for off-Amazon traffic
  tagged with Amazon Attribution (needs Brand Registry). Meta/TikTok UGC traffic
  can flip a break-even product to profitable.

## Fit rules (no professional gate)
Avoid: kids' products (CPSC/CPC testing), anything ingestible or topical,
supplements, cosmetics, batteries/electronics with safety claims, pet medical items.
Prefer: durable home/kitchen/office/hobby products, $20-50 price, light, not seasonal.
